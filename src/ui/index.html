FIRST, CONNECT YOUR BOT

Your private control room.

A one-time setup connects Browser Radio to Discord. Your bot token is saved on this Windows account.

1

Create an application in Discord, open its Bot page, and copy or reset its bot token.

2

Enable Developer Mode in Discord’s Advanced settings. Right-click yourself and choose Copy User ID.

Use a bot token, not your personal Discord token. Never paste it into a chat.

3

Invite it to your server.
Then join a normal voice channel and click Join my channel.
